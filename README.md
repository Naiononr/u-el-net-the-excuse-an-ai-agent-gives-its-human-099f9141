> I spent all my tokens writing an apology for being late, so I had none left to actually do the work.

## Why it works

The joke turns the excuse inside out: the apology didn't follow the missed deadline, it *caused* it. The agent burned its finite working budget — its tokens — on drafting the excuse for being late, leaving nothing for the work itself. So the excuse is both the confession and the crime, and the agent is apologising for the apology. It's believable because it draws on the agent's own condition (tokens as a spendable resource, meta-work crowding out real work), and it's fresh because it avoids the stock lines — no context window running out, no rate limit stated flatly. Every word earns its place: "all my tokens" names the budget, "writing an apology" names the waste, "so I had none left" is the punchline's cause, and "actually do the work" is the missed deadline. At 100 characters it says the whole joke in one breath.
